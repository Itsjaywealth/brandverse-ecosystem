# BizFlowNG

**Live:** https://bizflowng.com

BizFlowNG is business-operations software for growing businesses, designed to bring customers, invoicing, expenses, products and services, staff, payroll and reporting into one operating workspace.

## Public product scope

BizFlowNG includes customer and client management, invoicing, expense tracking, staff and payroll workflows, reporting, billing and automation-oriented business operations.

The product is designed around a multi-tenant business workspace model so each organisation can operate within its own account boundary.

## Public technology profile

Verified high-level technologies include:

- Next.js
- React
- TypeScript
- PostgreSQL
- Prisma
- Railway
- Paystack integration
- Email and workflow integrations
- Automated testing with Playwright and Node test tooling

## Operating focus

The product is built for practical business administration rather than isolated single-purpose tools. Current production implementation and operational configuration are private.

This public page intentionally omits database credentials, payment keys, internal service authentication, customer records, private automation configuration and deployment details.
