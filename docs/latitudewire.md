# LatitudeWire

**Live:** https://latitudewire.com

LatitudeWire is a digital publishing property within the BrandVerse ecosystem, covering international affairs, Africa, business, technology and other news and analysis categories.

## Public product scope

LatitudeWire provides a live publishing environment for editorial workflows, article presentation, categories, search visibility, performance and ongoing platform operations.

The project gives BrandVerse direct operating experience in content systems, publishing cadence, web performance, editorial presentation and platform maintenance.

## Public technology profile

- WordPress
- Custom theme and publishing configuration
- AWS-hosted infrastructure
- Search and web-performance operations

## Operating boundary

Editorial operations, private administrative access, infrastructure credentials, backups, analytics exports and internal publishing automation remain private.

The public portfolio documents the platform at a capability level only.
